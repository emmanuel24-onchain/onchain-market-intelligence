# On-Chain Market Intelligence

## Projects

### ### Launch Intelligence

An independent research and experimentation project exploring how on-chain market activity can be transformed into structured market intelligence.

The project combines on-chain data, behavioral analysis, research workflows, and technology to investigate crypto markets and develop tools around the research.
## THE PROBLEM 
Onchain Markets move quickly,and useful information is often scattered across markets data platforms,onchain analytics,social discussions,and news sources.For newer market participants,understanding these signals and distinguishing potential opportunities from risky situations can be difficult. LAUNCH INTELLIGENCE is being developed to help organise these information into a more structured research workflow.
## OVERVIEW 
LAUCNCH INTELLIGENCE is a crypto market intelligence project focused on onchain markets.it aims to turn market data,on-chain activity,and relevant research into structured,understandable insights that hep users evaluate potential opportunities and identify risks.The goal is to make onchain market research more accessible by translating complex signals into useful information rather than simply presenting unprocessed information.
## METHODOLOGY 
The project is being developed around ;
* Gather and organise relevant crypto market information.
* Support the evaluation of token activity and potential risks.
* classify research findings into structured intelligence categories.
* Deliver concise,readable market updates.
* Support continued monitoring as market conditions change.
